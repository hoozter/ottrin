# Prepared-claim sudden guest power observation

No production code changed. The exact raw test executable from e7cec9d4 was transferred without transformation and checked by SHA-256. An existing native fixture paused the real claim constructor at Prepared, after its complete first record and namespace flush but before publication. The ordinary actor made a separate foreign preparation and durably initialized source/public files.

The host verified a matching dispatch, live child executable and an age below 60 seconds, then saved readiness and original profile/install hashes. Only the owned COW domain with its expected single disk was stopped with virsh destroy. No guest shutdown, Rust destruction or guest handle cleanup ran. The host and physical storage stayed powered.

After one boot, a limited-account inspector checked the fixture before another Copy operation. The canonical key was absent. The unpublished preparation survived byte-identically as a complete first claim frame. Source, public sibling and foreign preparation hashes matched their pre-cut values. A separate native publisher then admitted the same final name with a fresh transaction and different native file identity. It preserved all four byte hashes and exited at the expected fixture code.

This observes non-blocking recovery and preserved fixture data for one completed-preparation boundary. It also demonstrates that process-exit delete-on-close is insufficient to collect every sudden-power leftover. No cleanup authority over that old file is inferred for the application; the harness removed its own guarded test root only after exporting the evidence.

The first attempt was not cut: the controller was prepared too late and its live-barrier age guard refused. The child subsequently ended normally, the task was verified terminal and the owned fixture was cleaned. Preparation and cut are now chained. The refusal is archived separately, not counted as power evidence.

Next work must address the observed preparation lifecycle and test earlier/partial, published/persisted and retirement power boundaries before broadening acceptance. Do not assume every leftover is a complete, independently bound record, and do not collect foreign entries from their name or age. Other filesystems, physical-host power, UI/installed-package and the full roadmap remain outside this experiment.
